# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

VU serves hiring teams, recruiters, and candidates moving through structured remote interviews. Visitors to the landing page need to understand the product quickly, trust that the interview process is controlled, and feel that the dashboard is operational rather than decorative.

## Product Purpose

VU is an AI-powered virtual interview platform that combines application intake, CV analysis, setup checks, adaptive interviews, integrity signals, scoring, transcripts, and review reports into one hiring workflow. Success means teams can make faster decisions with evidence they can defend, while candidates get a clear and calm interview experience.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, premium. The brand should feel technical and trustworthy without becoming generic AI SaaS decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI illustrations, random glowing shapes, stock-like dashboard cards, decorative motion without product meaning, over-rounded surfaces, and loud gradients. The landing page should not feel like a template or a disconnected marketing skin over the real dashboard.

## Design Principles

- Show the workflow before explaining it.
- Make every visual connect to a real VU product action or signal.
- Let brand orange/red attract attention because the surrounding neutrals are disciplined.
- Keep motion purposeful, subtle, and readable.
- Preserve dashboard credibility on marketing surfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect reduced-motion preferences, maintain readable contrast, keep product copy concise, and make interactive navigation/links remain usable with native browser behavior.
